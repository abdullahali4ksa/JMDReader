## 1. Overview
**JMDReader** is a modern, high-performance desktop application designed for reading, editing, and previewing Markdown (`.md`, `.markdown`) documents on Windows 10 and Windows 11. 

We strongly respect and value your privacy. **JMDReader does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, telemetry, usage statistics, or user files.**

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
* **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or device identifiers.
* **No File Contents:** All documents, markdown files, notes, and exported files are processed **100% locally on your machine**. Your files never leave your computer.
* **No Telemetry or Analytics:** The application contains zero third-party tracking SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no telemetry pings.
* **No Cloud Accounts or Logins:** JMDReader works completely offline without requiring any user registration, password, or third-party authentication.

---

## 3. Local File System Access
JMDReader interacts with your local storage only when you explicitly direct it to do so:
* **Opening a File:** When you choose *File -> Open* (or press `Ctrl+O`), or open a document via drag-and-drop or file association, JMDReader reads the contents solely to render them in your local viewer.
* **Saving / Editing:** When you save modifications, JMDReader writes only to the file path you have explicitly confirmed.
* **Exporting HTML / PDF:** Exported files are written directly to your chosen local destination.
* **User Preferences:** Application settings (such as theme choice, font size, language selection, and auto-scroll speed) are stored strictly on your local computer within your standard Windows AppData folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\MDReader`).

---

## 4. Internet Connectivity
JMDReader is designed to operate in completely **offline environments**:
* The built-in markdown parser, KaTeX mathematical formula renderer, Mermaid diagram engine, and typography styles are bundled locally within the app installation.
* The application makes **zero outbound internet network requests** during standard execution.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services
JMDReader does not embed third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or external APIs.

---

## 6. Compliance with Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR)
Because JMDReader collects no personal data whatsoever from any users, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).
